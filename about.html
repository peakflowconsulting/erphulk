Why ERP Hulk exists

Most ERP news is written for big companies or by the vendors themselves. If you run a business with 40 people, two warehouses and a finance team of three, almost none of it is about you.

ERP Hulk is the site we wanted when we started. We cover what changes in Zoho, Odoo, SAP Business One, NetSuite and the tools around them, and we explain it for owners and managers who have a business to run.

We're also honest about who we are. ERP Hulk is written by the team at Flowpeak Consulting. We're a Zoho partner, and we spend our days setting up CRM, Books, Inventory, People, Payroll and Creator for clients. That's where the playbooks and case studies come from. When we recommend something, it's because we've built it, broken it and fixed it.

No sponsored posts, no “top 10 ERP tools” lists stuffed with affiliate links. If we ever get paid to write about something, we'll say so at the top of the page.
