Why ERP Hulk exists

Most ERP news is written for big companies, or by the vendors themselves. If you run a business with 40 people, two warehouses and a finance team of three, almost none of it is about you.

ERP Hulk is written for you. We cover finance, stock and operations software: Zoho Books, Zoho Inventory and Zoho One, Odoo, Tally, SAP Business One, NetSuite, Dynamics 365 Business Central and ERPNext. We stick to ERP. CRM gets its own home at CRM Implementation Partners.

About the bias: ERP Hulk is written by Harikrishnan, who runs Flowpeak Consulting, a Zoho partner with more than 600 implementations behind it. So we know Zoho best, and we'll tell you when another ERP is the better fit.

No sponsored posts and no “top 10 ERP” lists stuffed with affiliate links. If we're ever paid to write about something, it will say so at the top of the page.
